# Campaign assets

The page renders exactly two white markup photo placeholders: Headshot here in the hero (10:11), and School Board photo here beside the School Board Member experience (3:2). See **Adding the two campaign photos** in the root README for exact paths, replacement markup, dimensions and alt text.

Blue `#003f7f` and yellow `#ffde59` were sampled from the supplied PDF's campaign graphic. Supporting dark blue and neutral surfaces are design additions. The favicon is a simple JL initials treatment. No stock or generated candidate imagery is used.
